Our story

More than 20 years in your community

Maplewood Family Practice was founded with one simple belief: that every patient deserves unhurried, thoughtful care. We are not a conveyor belt — we are your partners in health.

From newborns to grandparents, our team provides continuity of care across every chapter of your family's life. We combine evidence-based medicine with genuine human connection.

Bulk-billing and most medical aids accepted
